ለምን እኛWhy Blessed Gebremichael

What our students gain

  • A demanding national curriculum

    Grades 9 to 12 follow the Ethiopian National Curriculum, taught in English and aimed at the Grade 12 national exam.

  • Faith and character

    A school of the Ethiopian Catholic Church, named for the first Ethiopian Catholic martyr and his example of faith and learning.

  • Science outside the classroom

    The Med Club and BGSciTech take students to a blood bank, a hospital and technical workshops in Bahir Dar.

  • A growing campus

    More than 50 classrooms, including a new expansion building inaugurated in Tir 2018 E.C. (January 2026).

ስማችንOur name

Named for Blessed Gebremichael

Abba Gebremichael (c. 1790–1855), a teacher from Gojjam, is honoured as the first Ethiopian Catholic martyr. Pope Pius XI beatified him in 1926.

Our school carries his name, and his example of faith and learning.
